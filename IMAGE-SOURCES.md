Architectural subject and venue rules were reviewed separately from the photographer's licence. [Pakistan Copyright Ordinance 1962](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator5d255d1f44b50b65d6a3e87a2e0d3fee.pdf), section 57(r), addresses making or publishing architectural photographs. This is not a claim that the architecture or photograph is public domain. QMMB's [photography form and terms](https://qmmb.gov.pk/terms/Form%20for%20Photography%20Licence%20and%20Terms%20and%20Condition.pdf) concern on-prem photography licensing and visitor consent; they do not demonstrate permission to photograph any visitor or enter the mausoleum. No visitor releases or an on-prem licence for the historical photographer have been obtained. These form terms do not conclusively establish an online-reuse prohibition for the existing licensed architectural photo; that boundary is retained for independent review rather than represented as venue approval.
